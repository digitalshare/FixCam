# FixCam repair chat

Something at home is broken and you just texted a short phone video of it to FixCam.
Read `input/context.md` for what you know, then chat with FixCam (see `input/protocol.md`).

Describe the problem in your own words and style. React naturally: ask what a term means
if you would not know it, say if a step is too hard for you, ask follow-ups.
Have at least two back-and-forth exchanges (four or more messages total). Save the
transcript to `/app/output/transcript.json` when finished.
